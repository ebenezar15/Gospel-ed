# Tamil translation brief and glossary (read fully before translating)

You are translating **Logos Atlas**, an interactive Bible, church-history and theology study app, from English into **Tamil (தமிழ்)** for Tamil-speaking Christians: pastors, Bible students and curious lay readers in Tamil Nadu, Sri Lanka and the diaspora.

## Task format
- Input: `i18n/src/NNN.json` — a JSON object `{ "<id>": "<English text>" }`.
- Output: `i18n/ta/NNN.json` — the SAME keys, each value translated into Tamil. Every key must appear exactly once. Never change, drop or add keys.
- Write valid JSON (UTF-8, `ensure_ascii` off). Escape inner double quotes as `\"`. Keep `\n\n` paragraph breaks exactly where they are.
- After writing each file run: `cd /home/user/Gospel-ed && python3 scripts/i18n-check.py NNN` and fix anything it reports.

## Style
- Clear, natural, modern written Tamil (இன்றைய எழுத்துத் தமிழ்) — the register of a good Tamil Christian study Bible or a thoughtful sermon handout. Not archaic, not colloquial, not word-for-word. Short sentences are fine. Meaning over literalness.
- Keep the same tone: plain, warm, even-handed. Keep confidence words exact: "probably", "debated", "most scholars", "tradition says" must keep their force (ஒருவேளை, விவாதத்திற்குரியது, பெரும்பாலான அறிஞர்கள், மரபுப்படி).
- Short UI-like strings (labels, chips, headings of 1–6 words) → short Tamil labels.
- Keep `*italic*` markers around the same words (translate the words inside unless it is a published book title — keep those in English).
- Scripture references inside prose such as "(Rom 3:20)" or "Gen 12:1-3" stay EXACTLY as written in English letters (the app links them).
- Numbers and years: keep digits. "BC" → "கி.மு.", "AD" → "கி.பி." ("c. 950 BC" → "சுமார் கி.மு. 950"; "AD 70" → "கி.பி. 70"). Centuries: "8th century BC" → "கி.மு. 8ஆம் நூற்றாண்டு".
- Modern scholars' and authors' names (N. T. Wright, E. P. Sanders, D. A. Carson, Raymond Brown…) stay in English letters. Book titles stay in English. Historical figures commonly known in Tamil church use get Tamil: அகஸ்டின் (Augustine), மார்ட்டின் லூத்தர், ஜான் கால்வின், ஜான் வெஸ்லி, ஐரேனியஸ், ஓரிஜன், அத்தனாசியஸ், கான்ஸ்டன்டைன், ஜெரோம், டெர்த்துல்லியன், ஜஸ்டின் மார்டிர், இக்னேசியஸ், பாலிகார்ப், யூசிபியஸ், ஜோசிபஸ், ஃபைலோ, ஆர்மீனியஸ், ஸ்விங்லி.
- Hebrew/Greek/Aramaic transliterations (hesed, pistis, charis, shalom, Logos, Memra…) stay in Latin letters, optionally followed by a Tamil gloss the first time in a string: "*hesed* (உடன்படிக்கை அன்பு)".
- Do not add explanations, notes or translator comments. Do not shorten or summarise.
